Reading your own log

  • Fees column: for a free-entry project it should always read $0. If a "free" contest quietly starts charging, that number is your record that the rules changed after you entered.
  • Expected response: note the response time the venue states. If they blow past it, it's fair to query politely — and useful information for the next submission.
  • Exclusivity end dates: if a market's rights window is 6 months, put the date your rights return in "Expected response" and you have a self-managing reminder.
