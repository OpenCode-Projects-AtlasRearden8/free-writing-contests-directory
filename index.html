The directory

What this is: every entry below is free to enter or free to submit as of its review month listed on each card. There is nothing to sign up for on this site — no account, no email, no tracking. It works fully offline once loaded.
What this is not: not a guarantee, not legal or financial advice. Contest rules and magazine windows change constantly. Always verify at the official site linked on each card before submitting.

Where to find more free options

  • The Submission Grinder — a free, searchable database of paying markets.
  • Duotrope — a large market database (the service itself charges a small fee, but many listed markets are free to submit).
  • Ralan — long-running free listing of SF/F and horror markets, with paid/free status marked.
  • Horror Tree — free listings of horror-adjacent contests and anthology calls, with fee status called out.
  • University and library writing centers often list vetted local and student contests — usually free.

When you find one, run it past the red flags list and the pre-submission checklist before sending anything.

This directory is for education only and is not affiliated with any of the listed contests or magazines. Entry details, prizes, deadlines, and rights terms change; the linked official pages are the single source of truth. Use the filters and checks here as a starting point, never as permission to skip reading the rules.
