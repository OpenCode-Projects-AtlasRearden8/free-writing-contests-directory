Checklist

    Three habits that pay off

    1. Keep a log. One line per submission: what, where, when, their stated response time. The submission log page is built for this and stores everything on your own device.
    2. Save a copy of the rules page (print-to-PDF) on the day you submit. Rules pages sometimes change after the fact; a dated snapshot is your record of what you actually agreed to.
    3. Track exclusivity end dates. If a market wants 6 months of exclusive rights, note the date you can self-publish or send the piece elsewhere. The log's response-date column works for this too.

    Education only. This checklist reflects common industry practice, not legal advice; contract-specific questions belong with a publishing lawyer or an authors' association. Part of a free education project by Asdale Dynamics, LLC.
